Why use freeresizer?

Whether you need a profile photo at an exact size, a lighter picture for a website, or a PNG converted to JPG, freeresizer handles it in seconds. Because processing happens locally in your browser, your photos stay private and there is no waiting for uploads. The service is free and needs no account.

About freeresizer

freeresizer is a simple, free set of image tools built for people who just need a picture to be the right size. We believe basic image editing should be quick, private and free of sign-ups.

What we offer

  • Resize images by pixels or percentage
  • Enlarge small images and make memes
  • Add text and watermarks to images
  • Merge, split and make collages
  • View and remove image metadata (EXIF)
  • Pick colors, find duplicate images and clean up document scans
  • Convert photos to PDF and PDF pages to JPG
  • Resize to a target file size in KB or MB
  • Compress images to reduce file size
  • Rotate and flip images
  • Convert between JPG, PNG and WebP
